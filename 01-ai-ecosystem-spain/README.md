# Global AI Ecosystem Analysis: Spain's Strategic Position

## 📌 Project Overview
Artificial intelligence has become a strategic asset with significant economic, technological, and geopolitical impacts[cite: 3]. This project analyzes Spain's position within the global AI ecosystem using data from the Global AI Index[cite: 3]. By identifying strengths, weaknesses, and comparing performance against economically similar 'mirror countries', this analysis proposes an optimal investment strategy for public decision-makers[cite: 3].

## 🎯 Main Objectives
* **Global Evaluation:** Identify leading countries and understand their regional and income-level distribution[cite: 3].
* **Pillar Analysis:** Evaluate investment across 7 main pillars: Talent, Infrastructure, R&D, Operating Environment, Strategy, and Commercial[cite: 3].
* **Strategy Gap Detection:** Measure the gap between governmental strategies and actual R&D capabilities[cite: 3].
* **Benchmark Comparison:** Compare Spain with 'mirror countries' of similar economic size and demographics using derived metrics like *Intensity* and *Global_AI*[cite: 3].

## 🛠️ Technologies & Tools
* **Language:** Python[cite: 1]
* **Data Manipulation:** Pandas, NumPy[cite: 1]
* **Data Visualization:** Plotly (Express & Graph Objects) for interactive charts (Choropleth maps, Violin plots, Radars, and Diverging bar charts)[cite: 1]
* **Other Libraries:** Pycountry (for ISO3 standardizations)[cite: 1]

## 📊 Key Insights & Conclusions
* **Global Leaders:** The Americas are clearly led by the United States, while China stands out as the second global hub in the Asia-Pacific region[cite: 1]. 
* **The Strategy Gap:** Many countries have government strategies that are significantly more ambitious than their actual research and development capabilities[cite: 1].
* **Political Influence:** Liberal democracies concentrate the highest Total Score values, suggesting more favorable environments for research and innovation compared to closed autocracies[cite: 1].
* **Spain's Position:** When compared to similar-sized leaders like South Korea, Spain shows significant gaps in Infrastructure, Research, and Development, indicating a need to strengthen these specific areas[cite: 1].
