# Global AI Ecosystem Analysis: Spain's Strategic Position

## Project Overview
Artificial intelligence has become a strategic asset with significant economic, technological, and geopolitical impacts. This project analyzes Spain's position within the global AI ecosystem using data from the Global AI Index. By identifying strengths, weaknesses, and comparing performance against economically similar 'mirror countries', this analysis proposes an optimal investment strategy for public decision-makers.

## Main Objectives
* **Global Evaluation:** Identify leading countries and understand their regional and income-level distribution.
* **Pillar Analysis:** Evaluate investment across 7 main pillars: Talent, Infrastructure, R&D, Operating Environment, Strategy, and Commercial.
* **Strategy Gap Detection:** Measure the gap between governmental strategies and actual R&D capabilities.
* **Benchmark Comparison:** Compare Spain with 'mirror countries' of similar economic size and demographics using derived metrics like *Intensity* and *Global_AI*.

## Technologies & Tools
* **Language:** Python
* **Data Manipulation:** Pandas, NumPy
* **Data Visualization:** Plotly (Express & Graph Objects) for interactive charts (Choropleth maps, Violin plots, Radars, and Diverging bar charts)
* **Other Libraries:** Pycountry (for ISO3 standardizations)

## Key Insights & Conclusions
* **Global Leaders:** The Americas are clearly led by the United States, while China stands out as the second global hub in the Asia-Pacific region. 
* **The Strategy Gap:** Many countries have government strategies that are significantly more ambitious than their actual research and development capabilities.
* **Political Influence:** Liberal democracies concentrate the highest Total Score values, suggesting more favorable environments for research and innovation compared to closed autocracies.
* **Spain's Position:** When compared to similar-sized leaders like South Korea, Spain shows significant gaps in Infrastructure, Research, and Development, indicating a need to strengthen these specific areas.
